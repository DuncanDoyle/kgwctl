### Findings

- **P2 · correctness · DD-6 overstates the equivalence between `Labels` and `Resolve`.** They share the same merge function, but `Resolve` first filters to the highest claim certainty and `Labels` merges every claim it receives in `Kept`. DD-6 is accurate for kgwctl because both `Select` and `Narrow` call `readingsOf`, which filters to the highest certainty before building `Kept`. Downstream modules go through the same filter. With a manually constructed narrowing, an `Inferred` claim with unset indices and an agreeing `Ambiguous` claim with explicit `Inferred` indices would gain `(partly from name)` under `Labels`, while the old `normalise` would leave it off. That difference cannot reach kgwctl output through its command paths. D-036’s output claim holds for supported claims. Its last sentence would be clearer if it referred to the **merge** rule, since `notExact` still interprets unset as exact after establishing that the reading is exact.
